04 — Icon Anatomy
01
Graphite body — rounded rectangle (iOS superellipse, 23% radius). Top is noticeably lighter for visibility on dark home screens.
02
Warm screen glow — the ONLY color in the mark. Universal: an active screen is warm in every theme (Aurora, Magma, Cosmos…).
03
Letterbox bars — black strips top and bottom. Calculated from 2.35:1 aspect ratio. Makes the glowing rectangle read as "film playing."
04
Two silhouettes — head (circle) + shoulders (rounded rect). Color: "theater darkness" (#08090B). Figures are cut from light, not drawn on it.
05
Couch — one dense bar at bottom. Does not reach edges (iOS mask clips them). No legs or details — reads as couch at 60px.
06
Outer halo — warm radial gradient behind the icon. The mark stands in a projector beam. Without it, graphite disappears on dark backgrounds.
